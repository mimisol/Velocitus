# extension / retraction of blade

1 - pneumatic
    extend when less force is applied to the piston at the specific moment and lock in place with a locking bolt to take away stress from the piston.
    retraction requires less force, so unlocking the lock bolt and either by the user puting the foot down or with a little force from the piston in the reverse dirrection can retract the blade.

2 + solenoid
    fast and strong, can extend even if the user is standing, if the user is running and landing then it might be too much force but the implementation of some sort of ocking mechanism shoud be able to fix this problem.
    retracting should also be easy as then there is force from the user helping the blade retract and the solenoid on its own has enough force to do this.
